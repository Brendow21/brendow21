## Selected Projects

### [Stock Price Movement & Prediction](https://github.com/Brendow21/LSTM-Stock-Price-Prediction)
Tests whether historical prices and technical indicators (MA, EMA, MACD, Bollinger Bands) can predict next-day direction and future closing prices. It compares Logistic Regression and Random Forest classifiers with an LSTM regression model, and the results are written up as a research-style paper.
`Python` · `scikit-learn` · `LSTM` · `Jupyter`

### [Travel Buddy Finder](https://github.com/Brendow21/CS5614-Travel-Activity)
Full-stack Django app where users discover destinations, draft trips, search activities through the Google Places API, and connect with travelers who share their interests.
`Python` · `Django` · `Google Places API`

### [Artatouille](https://github.com/Brendow21/Artatouille)
VR experience for Meta Quest, built in Unity by a team of five.
`C#` · `Unity 6` · `Meta Quest`
